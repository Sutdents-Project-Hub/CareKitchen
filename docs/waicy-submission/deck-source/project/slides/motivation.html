WHAT INSPIRED US

We first imagined an advisor that checks drug–food interactions for older people. We could not do that safely. Underneath was a simpler, daily question: “What can I cook with what is at home, within my money, without breaking a limit I was given?”

Our assumption that this is a real daily friction has not yet been tested with older adults.
