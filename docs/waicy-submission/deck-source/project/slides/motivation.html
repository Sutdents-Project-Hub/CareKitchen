HOW OUR IDEA NARROWED

We first imagined an advisor that checks drug–food interactions for older people. We could not do that safely, so we focused on one daily question: “What can I cook with what is at home, within my money, without breaking a limit I was given?”

WHAT OLDER ADULTS TOLD RESEARCHERS

Cooking and shopping for one, food cost and help with food came up in UK focus groups (30 people aged 63–90; Whitelock & Ensaff, 2018). Community ties shaped food choices for 22 older Australians living alone (Low et al., 2020).

These themes became test cases: one serving, visible price gaps and a summary a relative can read. The studies did not evaluate CareKitchen.
