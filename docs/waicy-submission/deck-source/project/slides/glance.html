WHAT IT IS

A chat advisor for one home meal: checked options, short steps, reasons and a shopping summary that respects budget, equipment and confirmed restrictions.

WHAT IT IS NOT

Not a diagnosis, drug–food interaction checker, medical diet or live price service. It never guarantees that a meal is safe.

WHAT IS VERIFIED

V6 passes the 16-round English suite (59 / 59 checks), the 12 targeted behaviors and 62 automated tests. Not yet studied with real older adults.
