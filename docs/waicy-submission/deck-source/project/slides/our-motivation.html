MOTIVATION · WHY WE STARTED

“What should we eat today?” is not a simple question

THE EVERYDAY CHALLENGE

As society ages, older people’s daily meals and care deserve attention. For an older adult living alone, one meal can mean weighing food at home, money, cooking ability, taste and dietary restrictions at once.

WHERE HELP FALLS SHORT

Online recipes and generative AI offer ideas that may not fit real life: food that is not at home, an ignored budget, or conditions lost after a change. Complex screens can be hard, and family cannot plan meals every day.

OUR IDEA

Combine a large language model with condition checks, so older adults, relatives and caregivers can describe their situation in everyday language and get meals that fit their food, budget, equipment and confirmed restrictions.

WHAT WE HOPE FOR

An AI that does more than answer: it understands needs, remembers important conditions, asks when information is missing, and avoids unsupported prices or medical safety promises. We hope to ease daily meal decisions, reduce food waste, and make technology an easier helper for older adults and the families who care for them.
