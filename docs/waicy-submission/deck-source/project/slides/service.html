THE SPECIFIC AI SERVICE · AI AS A SERVICE

What CareKitchen does, and for whom

CORE FUNCTION

A home-meal advisor for one meal at a time: 2–3 checked options, short steps, reasons on request, price status and a shareable shopping summary.

PROBLEM

Deciding what to cook from what is at home, within a small purchase budget and the available equipment, without breaking confirmed restrictions.

AUDIENCE

An older adult cooking alone, a family caregiver, and a relative who plans and shops from a distance.

VALUE

Conditions persist and are re-checked after every change. Unknown prices stay unknown. Medical questions are referred, never answered.

EXPERTISE

Home meals under budget, equipment and confirmed restrictions, from a fixed catalog of 21 recipes (prices in NT$).

ROLE OF THE AI

Reads intent, ranks eligible meals by preference and role, and flags what is unclear. The server keeps conditions, checks them and calculates costs.

OUT OF SCOPE

Diagnosis, drug–food interactions, medical diets, live prices and nutrient claims.
