02 · THE SPECIFIC AI SERVICE

What CareKitchen does, and for whom

CORE FUNCTION

A chat advisor that proposes 2–3 feasible meal directions for one meal, with short cooking steps and a shopping summary.

PROBLEM

Deciding what to cook from what is at home, within a small purchase budget and the available equipment, without breaking confirmed restrictions.

AUDIENCE

An older adult cooking alone, a family caregiver, and a relative who plans and shops from a distance.

VALUE

Conditions persist and are re-checked after every change. Unknown prices stay unknown. Medical questions are referred, never answered.

EXPERTISE

Everyday home meal planning under budget, equipment and user-confirmed restrictions (Taiwan context, prices in NT$).

ROLE OF THE AI

Reads free-form Chinese requests, follows changes across turns, and ranks and explains meals from a project-written library. The server enforces the rules.

OUT OF SCOPE

Diagnosis, drug–food interactions, medical diets, live prices and nutrient claims.
