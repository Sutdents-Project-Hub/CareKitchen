LIMITATIONS

What CareKitchen cannot yet do or claim

No real users yet

Every case is invented and each version ran once. Browser “users” were AI agents. Pass counts are engineering checks, not proof of user success.

Small meal library

With soy excluded, suggestions shrink to plain staples such as steamed potatoes. Dishes reflect one food culture.

Understanding can fail

Conditions are read by rules. In a live check, “an oven” was saved as an ingredient; the app then asked for confirmation and gave no meals.

Not a clinical tool

No nutrient values and no allergen cross-contact guarantee. Processed foods may hide ingredients.

Language and access

English only, while older people near us mostly use Chinese or Taiwanese. Large text exists, but no accessibility audit.

Prices and reliability

Prices come only from the user. The service depends on an external model; one unexplained error occurred in testing.
