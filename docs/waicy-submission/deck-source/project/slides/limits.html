LIMITATIONS

What CareKitchen cannot yet do or claim

No user study yet

Results come from prepared test cases, one run per version. A study with older adults, caregivers and relatives has not yet been carried out.

Small meal library

21 recipes from 14 ingredients, with no meat or fish. Given chicken and rice, it can only say it has no matching dish.

Understanding can fail

Rules read the conditions. On 28 November, “two eggs” was saved as a food name, and “I am 75 and live alone” did not set one serving.

Not a clinical tool

No nutrient values and no allergen cross-contact guarantee. Processed foods may hide ingredients.

Language and access

English only, while older people near us mostly use Chinese or Taiwanese. Large text exists, but no accessibility audit.

Prices and reliability

Prices come only from the user. The service depends on an external model; one unexplained error occurred in testing.
