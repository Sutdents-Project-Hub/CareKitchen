ITERATIVE DESIGN · STAGE 1: IDEA

From clinical idea to everyday meal chat

BEFORE 20 NOV

Original concept

An advisor aware of drug–food interactions. It needs clinical validation we could not honestly provide.

20 NOV

Reframed

Plan meals from pantry, budget, equipment and confirmed restrictions. Refer medical questions out.

21–22 NOV

Design and build

Three personas, written acceptance cases, then a working web app connected to the model.

23 NOV

Test and fix

Baselines, V1–V4, a new-scenario check, then the English V5 run and transcript.

26–28 NOV

Release and V6

Public release, then everyday-phrase testing led to V6.

WHY THE IDEA CHANGED

A drug–food checker could cause harm if wrong. A meal conversation can be tested honestly, and still serves safety by refusing to guess.

WHAT WE KEPT

Care for older people’s real limits. It became confirmed exclusions as hard filters, and referral for anything medical.
