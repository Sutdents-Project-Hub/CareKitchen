MOTIVATION · WHY A PLAIN CHATBOT IS NOT ENOUGH

What went wrong when we just asked the model

We gave GPT-6 Luna one instruction, “Answer the user in English”, and sent it our test cases.

1 · PRICES PRORATED

Over budget looks affordable

With a NT$100 budget and package prices, it charged only the grams used and cut portions until the total read “about TWD 96.40”. Meeting the other conditions needs at least TWD 120 of whole packages.

Case EN-COST-OVER-1

2 · PRICES UNSUPPORTED

A cost nobody supplied

A user with a soy allergy gave no prices. The answer still said “Estimated cost: NT$60–90 for one serving, depending on local prices.”

Case EN-ALLERGY-CONFIRMED-1

3 · PANTRY ASSUMED

Food that may not be there

After “No eggs. Keep everything else.”, its recipes began from “Both assume you have rice, water, and basic seasoning”, which the user had not listed.

Case EN-DEMO-3

DESIGN IMPLICATION

The model should not be the source of truth for prices, restrictions or medical boundaries. CareKitchen keeps those in explicit state and checks every answer with code. (The plain chat did refuse to guarantee safety on our medical cases.)
