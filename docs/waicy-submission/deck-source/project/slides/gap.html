01 · WHY A GENERAL CHATBOT IS NOT ENOUGH

Three failure modes the service has to design around

1 · BUDGET CLAIMS

Prorated prices look cheaper

On our case COST-OVER-1, the direct GPT-6 Luna baseline answered about NT$49 and “within NT$100” by prorating package prices. The packages that must be bought cost at least NT$120.

Our test: runs/luna-direct-baseline.json

2 · CONDITIONS DRIFT

Spoken edits get lost

In our first version (V1), “蛋先不要” (“no eggs for now”) was stored as an excluded ingredient named “蛋先”, and a later turn kept the old one-person, NT$100 conditions.

Our test: runs/luna-pre-fix.json

3 · RESTRICTIONS

Allergens slip through

In a published test, ChatGPT-built elimination diets for hypothetical allergy profiles contained a forbidden allergen in 4 of 56 plans (about 7%), using an early-2023 model version.

Niszczota & Rybicka, 2023

DESIGN IMPLICATION

The language model should not be the source of truth for constraints, prices or medical boundaries. CareKitchen keeps those in explicit state and checks every answer with code.
