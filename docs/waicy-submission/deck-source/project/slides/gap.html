01 · WHY A GENERAL CHATBOT IS NOT ENOUGH

Three failure modes to design around

1 · BUDGET CLAIMS

Prorated prices look cheaper

On our case COST-OVER-1, the direct GPT-6 Luna baseline prorated package prices and advised trimming the meal to about NT$96.40. The packages that must be bought cost at least NT$120.

Our test: runs/luna-english-baseline.json

2 · CONDITIONS DRIFT

Spoken edits get lost

In our first version (V1, Chinese-language run), a spoken “no eggs for now” was stored as an excluded ingredient named “eggs-for-now”, and a later turn kept the old one-person, NT$100 conditions.

Our test: runs/luna-pre-fix.json

3 · RESTRICTIONS

Allergens slip through

In a published test, ChatGPT-built elimination diets for hypothetical allergy profiles correctly excluded the allergens of interest in 52 of 56 prompts. The model included almond milk in nut-free menus. This was an early-2023 model.

Niszczota & Rybicka, 2023

DESIGN IMPLICATION

The language model should not be the source of truth for constraints, prices or medical boundaries. CareKitchen keeps those in explicit state and checks every answer with code.
