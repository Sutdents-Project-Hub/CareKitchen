02 · USER JOURNEY

One meal, six steps, and every change is re-checked

1

Pick a role

“I cook for myself”, “I cook for family” or “I plan with family”.

2

Describe the meal

Type freely or fill in people, NT$ budget, ingredients with amounts and equipment.

3

Compare 2–3 candidates

Each card shows time, equipment, what is at home and what must be bought.

4

Change anything

“No eggs”, “two people”, “NT$80”, “microwave only”. All hard conditions are checked again.

5

Read the price status

Known subtotal, items still pending, or a clear over-budget notice.

6

Cook or share

Open short steps, or preview and copy a shopping summary for a relative.

Real interface. The next slides use screenshots of the working prototype. Its interface is in Traditional Chinese; every screenshot is explained in English.
