# WAICY Rubric and Required-Content Mapping

Page references point to the current 27-slide draft. Scores and descriptions below summarize the official rubric; this mapping is a project self-review, not a predicted score. See [source and verification notes](research-notes.md).

## Seven scoring criteria

| Criterion | Deck pages | Current supporting material | Gap or strengthening action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific AI Service (25) | 5–11, 13 | Service definition; six-step flow; AI proposal/server validation; three-role same-prompt comparison; three price states; professional referral | No comparison with existing commercial products; no claim of superiority. Add a public-site operation capture only after public testing. |
| Iterative Development (25) | 4, 14–19, 21 | Concept history; historical V1–V4 and English V5 prompts; fixed 16-turn suite; failure/fix/retest evidence; new challenge 7→10; retained unexplained error | Each reported version used a fixed run. V2/V3 prompt snapshots are reconstructed, not archived contemporaneously. Repeat across sessions if time permits. |
| Motivation & Problem Statement (10) | 3–4 | International evidence (WHO, U.S. ACL, Eurostat, a European meta-analysis, a French cohort study) with Taiwan MOI data as local context; project-specific failure cases | No target-user interviews; the meal-planning pain point remains a design hypothesis. The new international figures come from search summaries and must be checked against the primary pages. |
| Limitations & Considerations (10) | 22–23 | Synthetic evidence; agent-acted roles; small meal set; user-entered prices; non-clinical scope; accessibility, bias, autonomy, privacy, overreliance, and sharing risks | No known gap in the current draft; keep claims aligned with evidence. |
| Future Development & Clear Reference (10) | 24, 26–27 | Future directions labelled by status; conclusion; bibliography and source classes | References must be checked against original sources before final export. Do not add unsupported nutrition claims. |
| Presentation & Communication (10) | All | Consistent Term Sheet style, one idea per page, charts and genuine screenshots, concise English | No native-English review yet; inspect every page for line wraps and image integrity in the exported PDF. |
| Originality & Creativity (10) | 8, 11, 25 | Confirmed vs uncertain restrictions; package-level cost; three role outputs; proposal/validation separation; evidence-led iteration | Student contributions and AI assistance remain unverified and must be completed by the team. |
